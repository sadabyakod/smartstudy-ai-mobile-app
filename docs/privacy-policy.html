Privacy Policy
Last updated: December 18, 2025

SmartStudy ("we", "our", or "us") is a study support application designed to help students access learning materials and practice educational content. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only minimal information required for the app to function:

  • User Inputs: Text entered by users to receive study-related responses. This data is processed temporarily and is not stored after the session ends.
  • Device Information: Basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics to understand how the app is used and to improve performance.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used only to:

  • Provide study-related responses and educational support
  • Improve app functionality and performance
  • Fix errors and improve user experience

3. Educational Content Notice

SmartStudy provides educational content and practice material for general learning purposes only. The content is intended to support study and revision and should not be considered official or certified educational material.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share personal information with third parties. Information may be processed by trusted service providers solely for app functionality and under strict confidentiality.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

SmartStudy is suitable for students, including children under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation and does not collect personal information such as full names, phone numbers, email addresses, or precise location data from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been shared by a child, they may contact us to request deletion of the information.

7. User Rights

You may request:

  • Access to information related to your usage
  • Correction or deletion of data, where applicable

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
