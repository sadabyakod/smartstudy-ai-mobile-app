Privacy Policy
Last Updated: December 2025

SmartStudy ("we", "our", or "us") is an educational study support application for school and college students in India. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe learning environment for all users, including children.

Key Privacy Facts:

  • We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data
  • No account creation or login required
  • No advertisements
  • No social features or user interactions
  • Safe for children under 13

1. Information We Collect

SmartStudy collects minimal, non-personal information to provide educational services:

  • Study Queries: Questions or text you enter to receive educational responses. This information is processed temporarily to provide answers and is not permanently stored or linked to your identity.
  • Technical Information: Anonymous technical data such as device type, operating system version, and app version to ensure compatibility and improve performance.
  • Usage Statistics: Anonymous app usage patterns to understand how features are used and to improve the learning experience.

We do not collect: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location data, photos, contacts, or any other personal information.

2. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used solely to:

  • Provide educational content and study support
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Fix technical issues and optimize performance
  • Analyze anonymous usage patterns to enhance features

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information. SmartStudy may use essential third-party services to provide educational functionality. These services process information solely to support app features and are bound by strict confidentiality requirements.

Information may only be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and our services.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmission is encrypted, and we follow industry best practices to maintain data security.

5. Children's Privacy (Google Play Families Policy Compliance)

SmartStudy is designed to be safe for children under 13 years of age. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

  • The app does not require account creation or login
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • No advertisements are displayed
  • There are no social features, chat functions, or user interactions
  • No links to external websites or social media
  • Content is educational and age-appropriate

Parental Rights: If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided any information through the app, or if you have any concerns about your child's privacy, please contact us immediately at sadabyakod@gmail.com. We will promptly address your concerns and delete any information if requested.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about data processing
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your usage
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Contact us with questions or concerns about privacy

7. Data Retention

Study queries and responses are processed temporarily and are not permanently stored. Anonymous technical and usage data may be retained for app improvement purposes only and cannot be linked to individual users.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Compliance

SmartStudy complies with:

  • Google Play Families Policy
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • Information Technology Act, 2000 (India)
  • Applicable data protection and privacy laws
